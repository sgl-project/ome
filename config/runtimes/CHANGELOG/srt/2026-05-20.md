### Known Limitations

- The full standard-scenario concurrency matrix is bounded to 1 minute / 100 requests per row. The D(128000,200)
  stress scenario remains targeted at concurrency 8 and concurrency 4 because the all-concurrency 128k sweep is impractical.
- The D(128000,200) concurrency 8 runs completed with zero errors but output speed was below 10 tokens/sec/request for
  both images. The concurrency 4 retries completed with zero errors and above 10 tokens/sec/request.
- Version comparison differences were token-level mismatches only, with no missing baseline outputs.
