### Known issues
- vllm adds non-english characters (Ġ) to output, as observed with sanity checks. This is a non-issue for Llama-Guard but should be resolved for other models. 
- Llama Guard cannot serve more than 5 images per request without dropoff in accuracy (per its model card).
